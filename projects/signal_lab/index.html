ENGINEERING PROJECT / ELECTRONICS

Arduino Signal Lab

Investigating electronic components through circuit analysis, measurements and experimental data.

Status: In progress

Project Overview

The goal is to develop a small electronics test bench using an Arduino Uno, basic electronic components and a multimeter. The experiments investigate circuit behaviour and compare theoretical predictions with measured results.

Objectives

  • Investigate the forward voltage of a 1N4001 diode.
  • Measure the charging response of an RC circuit.
  • Collect voltage data using the Arduino.
  • Compare experimental results with circuit theory.
  • Identify sources of error and possible improvements.

Components and Equipment

  • Arduino Uno
  • 1N4001 rectifier diode
  • 10 kΩ resistor
  • 1 µF capacitor
  • Digital multimeter
  • Arduino data logging and serial tools

Additional resistors and the 741CN operational amplifier may be used in separate experiments after their circuits and operating limitations are checked.

Experiment 1: Diode Characterisation

A forward-biased diode is tested with a series resistor. The voltage across the diode is measured and the current is estimated using Ohm's law.

Calculation:

I = (Vsupply - Vdiode) / R

The diode voltage depends on current, temperature and the particular component. Measurements must be recorded before conclusions are drawn.

Experiment 2: RC Charging Response

A resistor and capacitor are used to investigate how capacitor voltage changes over time.

Time constant:

τ = RC

For a 10 kΩ resistor and a 1 µF capacitor, the nominal time constant is 10 ms.

Theoretical charging equation:

Vc(t) = Vs(1 - e^(-t/RC))

The measured response will be compared with the theoretical curve, accounting for sampling rate, component tolerance and measurement limitations.
